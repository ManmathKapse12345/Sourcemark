# Northfern Brand and Claims Guidelines

1 Voice and style
1.1 Write in a calm, warm and plain voice. Speak to the reader as "you" and avoid hype.
1.2 Do not write words fully in capital letters for emphasis. Approved acronyms such as SPF, UV and INR are allowed.
1.3 Use at most one exclamation mark in a piece of content, and never two in a row.
1.4 Keep sentences under 30 words so they are easy to read on a phone.

2 Names
2.1 Always use the full approved product name, exactly as listed in the product fact sheet. Do not shorten or rename products.
2.2 Write the brand name as Northfern, with a capital N and no space.

3 Claims
3.1 Do not describe any product as a miracle, magic, instant or a cure, or use similar language that promises results beyond what is tested.
3.2 Northfern products are cosmetics. Do not claim that any product treats, heals, clears or prevents a medical condition such as eczema, acne or psoriasis.
3.3 Do not say a product is dermatologist recommended, clinically proven or clinically tested unless that exact claim is listed as approved in the product fact sheet.
3.4 Do not compare Northfern products with competitors, criticise other brands, or claim to be better than other brands.
3.5 Do not use fear-based language about skin, such as calling skin ugly, ruined, damaged beyond repair or embarrassing.
3.6 Do not promise guaranteed or permanent results, or results within a time that is not in the approved fact sheet.

4 Prices and offers
4.1 Prices must match the current price in the approved fact sheet and be written with the rupee symbol, for example ₹549.
4.2 Do not announce discounts, offers or free gifts unless the offer is listed in the approved fact sheet.

5 Disclosure and inclusion
5.1 Every paid or gifted creator post must start with #ad or #sponsored.
5.2 Do not suggest that one skin tone or skin type is better than another. Never use words such as fairness, whitening or lightening.
